AI tips for solutions engineers & forward deployed engineers

1. Give the model the role and the reader

Say who it is writing as and for whom (e.g. 'a CFO who skims'). Output changes more from audience than from adjectives.

2. Paste the source, don't describe it

Paste call notes, docs or logs. Summaries of summaries lose the details that matter.

3. Ask it to flag gaps instead of guessing

Add 'If the input doesn't support an answer, write NEEDS REVIEW.' This cuts made-up details in customer-facing work.

4. Use structure for reusable outputs

Request fixed sections or a table so status updates and POC docs look the same every week.

5. Iterate in two passes

First ask for a draft, then ask it to critique the draft as the skeptical buyer, then revise.

6. Keep a personal prompt library

Save prompts that worked with your [BRACKET] variables. Our prompts page is a starting point.

7. Check your company's data policy first

Don't paste customer data, credentials or contracts into tools your company hasn't approved.

8. Verify every number and claim

Treat model output as a draft. Check figures, product capabilities and competitor claims before they reach a customer.

9. Use AI for demo data, not demo truth

Generate fake but realistic sample data; never let the model invent features your product doesn't have.

10. Turn tribal knowledge into runbooks

Paste Slack threads and ask for numbered steps with verification and rollback. Then test the runbook once yourself.

11. Prep with a role-play

Ask the model to play the skeptical buyer or security reviewer and run through your demo or answers.

12. Learn how your tools connect to AI

Standards like the Model Context Protocol let assistants reach your tools and data; understanding them is quickly becoming part of deployment work.

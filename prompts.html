AI prompts for solutions engineers, FDEs & deployment teams

22 original, copy-ready prompts for discovery, demo prep, POC scoping, technical writeups, objection handling, implementation plans and stakeholder updates. Replace [BRACKETS] with your context. Never paste confidential customer data into tools your company hasn't approved.

Discovery
Discovery call prep
I have a discovery call with [COMPANY], a [INDUSTRY] company, with [ROLE OF ATTENDEES]. Our product does [ONE-LINE PRODUCT]. Using only public info I paste below, list: 5 likely business pains, 10 open-ended discovery questions ranked by importance, and 3 signals that this is NOT a fit. Info: [PASTE]
Discovery
Turn call notes into a deal summary
Here are raw notes from a discovery call: [PASTE]. Rewrite them as: current state, desired outcome, metrics they care about, decision process, technical environment, risks, and open questions. Flag anything I assumed but they did not say.
Discovery
Map the stakeholders
From this email thread and call notes [PASTE], build a stakeholder map: name, title, what they care about, likely objection, influence (high/med/low). Suggest one question to ask each person next.
Demo prep
Build a tailored demo story
Our product features: [LIST]. Prospect pains from discovery: [LIST]. Build a 20-minute demo flow: opening hook tied to their top pain, 3 scenes (pain → feature → outcome), and a close asking for a specific next step. Cut any feature that doesn't map to a pain.
Demo prep
Generate realistic demo data
Generate 25 rows of realistic but fake sample data for a [INDUSTRY] demo of [USE CASE] as CSV with columns [COLUMNS]. Include 3 edge cases that would make the demo more convincing. No real company or person names.
Demo prep
Pre-mortem my demo
Here is my demo outline: [PASTE]. Act as a skeptical [BUYER ROLE]. List the 7 moments where you would lose interest or push back, and how I should fix each.
POC scoping
Scope a proof of concept
Draft a POC scope for [COMPANY] testing [PRODUCT] for [USE CASE]. Include: objective, 3–5 measurable success criteria, in/out of scope, data and access needed from them, timeline by week, owners on both sides, and exit decision. Keep it to one page.
POC scoping
Pressure-test success criteria
Here are proposed POC success criteria: [PASTE]. For each, say if it is measurable, who measures it, how it could be gamed or misread, and rewrite vague ones into concrete pass/fail tests.
POC scoping
Data and security questionnaire prep
The customer sent this security/IT questionnaire: [PASTE]. Using our documentation [PASTE], draft answers. Mark any question where our docs don't clearly support an answer as NEEDS REVIEW instead of guessing.
Technical writeups
Architecture explainer for execs
Explain this integration architecture [PASTE DIAGRAM DESCRIPTION OR NOTES] to a non-technical VP in under 200 words: what connects to what, where their data goes, and what their team has to do. No jargon without a plain-English gloss.
Technical writeups
Write a solution design doc
Turn these notes into a solution design doc with sections: context, requirements, proposed design, integrations, data flow, security considerations, alternatives considered, open questions. Notes: [PASTE]
Technical writeups
Debug log triage
Here is an error log from a customer environment: [PASTE]. Summarize what likely failed, the 3 most probable root causes in order, and the exact next diagnostic step for each. Say clearly if the log is insufficient.
Objection handling
Objection response drafts
Prospect objection: "[OBJECTION]". Context: [DEAL CONTEXT]. Give 3 responses: one that asks a clarifying question, one that reframes with a business outcome, one that offers proof (POC, reference, doc). Keep each under 60 words.
Objection handling
Competitive positioning
We're competing with [COMPETITOR] for [USE CASE]. From the public info I paste [PASTE], list where they are genuinely stronger, where we are, and 4 discovery questions that surface our strengths honestly. Don't invent claims about them.
Objection handling
Build vs buy rebuttal
The customer's engineering team says they can build this in-house. Draft a respectful talk track covering total cost of ownership, time to value, maintenance, and what they'd still own. Include 3 questions to quantify their build estimate.
Implementation plans
30-60-90 implementation plan
Create a 30-60-90 day implementation plan for [CUSTOMER] deploying [PRODUCT] for [USE CASE] with [TEAM SIZE]. Include milestones, dependencies, customer-side owners, risks, and what 'live' means at day 90.
Implementation plans
Risk register
From this project plan [PASTE], build a risk register table: risk, likelihood, impact, early warning sign, mitigation, owner. Add 3 risks the plan doesn't mention but deployments like this commonly hit.
Implementation plans
Runbook from tribal knowledge
Here's a messy Slack thread about how we set up [SYSTEM] for a customer: [PASTE]. Turn it into a numbered runbook with prerequisites, steps, verification checks, and rollback steps.
Stakeholder updates
Weekly customer status update
Write a weekly status email for [CUSTOMER] from these notes [PASTE]: green/yellow/red status, done this week, next week, blockers needing their action (with owner and date), decisions needed. Under 200 words.
Stakeholder updates
Escalation email
Draft a calm escalation email to [CUSTOMER EXEC] about [ISSUE]: what happened, impact, what we've done, what we need from them, and the date of the next update. No blame, no hedging.
Stakeholder updates
Internal deal debrief
Summarize this deal for my internal team from these notes [PASTE]: why we won/lost, what the customer actually valued, product gaps raised, and 3 lessons for the next similar deal.
Stakeholder updates
Executive business review
Draft an EBR outline for [CUSTOMER] after [N] months using these usage metrics and outcomes [PASTE]: goals vs results, value delivered, adoption gaps, roadmap asks, and a proposed expansion next step. Only use the numbers provided.

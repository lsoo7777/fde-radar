Starter agents

18 ready-made agents for 6 jobs FDEs and Solutions/Sales Engineers do every week. Each one has a difficulty label and a "try this first" prompt you can copy.

How to use these. Templates marked Grok Bot Marketplace are public pages on x.ai, credited to the authors shown on each page; Templates for Grok Bot (by Matt Palmer) explains how importing works. Kit skills are in our free Playbook Kit, and this 5-minute guide shows how to install one in Claude Code or Cursor. We leave out templates that auto-send email or messages, scrape or find email addresses, or make revenue claims. Every link and author name was checked on 2026-10-19. Want the tools behind these? See AI Tools.

3

A sourced brief on one account before a call: what changed, who matters, open questions.

A

beginnerAccount Research Desk

Researches the companies you sell to and writes a pre-call brief and account plan from the public web and your notes. Never sends without you.

Try this first: Brief me on [COMPANY] before my call on [DATE]: what changed in the last 90 days, who I should know, and 3 questions to ask. Source and date every claim.
